**What is Raku?**

**Raku** is a modern, expressive, general purpose programming language.

Originally developed under the name Perl 6, Raku has evolved into an
independent language with its own name, compiler implementations,
community, and ecosystem.

Raku provides a broad collection of programming tools, including
gradual typing, multiple dispatch, powerful regular expressions
and grammars, Unicode support by default, concurrency, object-oriented
programming, and functional programming features.

Many of the projects discussed in Raku Ramblings, including 
Checkwriter, are written in Raku.
